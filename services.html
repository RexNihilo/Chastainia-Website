Services

Business systems built around real work, not software checklists.

ChastainIA helps small businesses organize leads, customers, quoting, documents, approvals, operations, and reporting into practical systems people can maintain.

01

CRM Implementation

Organize leads, customers, opportunities, communications, and follow-ups so sales activity is visible and consistent. The goal is knowing who needs attention, what was promised, and what happens next.

02

Quoting and Configuration Systems

Build guided estimating tools with product options, pricing rules, quote documents, discount controls, and approval workflows.

03

Workflow Automation

Automate routing, reminders, document creation, email workflows, e-signatures, approval requests, and handoffs between sales and operations.

04

Operations Management

Track job status, installation or service work, procurement, scheduling, internal handoffs, and customer updates.

05

Reporting and Dashboards

Create useful visibility into pipeline, revenue, backlog, workload, business activity, and operational status without burying users in noise.

06

Custom Applications

Some businesses have workflows that generic software handles poorly. ChastainIA can build focused tools when a tailored system is more practical.

07

Process and Systems Consulting

Before building, we can map the current process, clarify ownership, remove unnecessary work, and choose the right platform. Salesforce may be part of the solution, but it is treated as a tool, not the strategy.
