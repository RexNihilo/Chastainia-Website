About

Useful systems over unnecessary complexity.

ChastainIA is an owner-led consultancy for small businesses that need better ways to manage work, customers, data, documents, and decisions.

Background

Built from operations discipline and technical delivery.

ChastainIA is led by Carey Chastain, a U.S. Coast Guard veteran with a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from The Citadel and professional experience in data engineering, analytics, systems integration, and project delivery.

That background matters for small businesses because the work is rarely just software. The real job is understanding how people sell, communicate, approve, schedule, document, and complete work, then building a system that supports that reality.

The preference is simple: build useful, maintainable solutions that reduce confusion and manual work without creating more complexity than the business can support.

Technical enough to build

Data, integrations, automation, reporting, and custom tools can be handled directly.

Practical enough to simplify

The best system is the one users can understand, maintain, and rely on after launch.

Small enough to stay close

Owner-led work means the person understanding the process is also close to the implementation.

Work together

Start with a real operational problem.

Bring the process that is manual, unclear, or hard to manage. We will talk through what should happen next.
